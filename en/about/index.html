About me

Design, photography and clear visual communication.

I’m Andrii Laska, a digital and print media design apprentice in Germany. My focus is graphic design, photography, web design and developing visual concepts.

I’m interested in how design, imagery and clear communication work together. My work combines visual intuition with structure, from print and advertising campaigns to photography and straightforward websites.

This portfolio presents selected work from my apprenticeship alongside personal photography projects. It continues to grow and documents my development as a media designer.
